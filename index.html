📘 Monthly Planner — Hindi Guide

Yeh tool aapko poore mahine ki planning ek jagah karne me madad karta hai — bina kisi login ya internet connection ke. Sab data sirf aapke browser me save hota hai.

1. Calendar Kaise Use Karein

Kisi bhi date par click karke us din ke saare tasks dekh sakte hain. Har category ka alag color-dot dikhta hai — Work (violet), Personal (green), Finance (gold), Health (red).

2. Tasks Add Karna

Right side me task likhein, category aur priority choose karein, date select karein aur "Task Add Karein" dabayein. Checkbox par click karke task complete mark kar sakte hain.

3. Monthly Goals

Bade goals (jaise "5 naye clients lena" ya "₹50,000 revenue") set karein aur unhe complete hone par tick karein.

4. Progress Track Karna

Progress Overview section me dikhega ki aapne kitne % tasks aur goals complete kiye hain — motivation ke liye.

5. Notes

Koi bhi important reminder ya idea Notes box me likh dein, wo automatically save ho jaayega.

6. Export/Import & Print

Export button se apna data JSON file me backup le sakte hain, Import se wapas load kar sakte hain, aur Print se pura planner print/PDF kar sakte hain.

Is tool ko apne CA firm clients ya local business owners ko free deke unhe premium version (reminders, WhatsApp sync, team sharing) ka upsell karein — Fiverr/Upwork par "Monthly Business Planner Setup" gig bana kar bhi sell kar sakte hain.
